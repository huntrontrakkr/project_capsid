Shell state graphs

The 64 states of a six-node hexagonal shell, joined by one-unit changes (the 6-cube) and grouped by rotation into 14 necklaces. Each page is self-contained and works offline. Crossing counts are unit-edge crossings computed by shell_graph.py.

  1. Necklaces as polygonsFirst sketch: rotation orbits drawn as hexagons, triangles and pairs in rows by fill level, with tap-to-route and a dominance toggle.
  2. Six-cube projectionsThe plain Hamming cube, layered by fill level or in the 12-gon (Petrie) projection.
  3. 12-gon colored by necklaceComplementary necklaces share a hue and sit point-symmetric about the center.
  4. Layered view colored by necklaceSame coloring in rows; toggle animates between the two projections.
  5. Necklace rowsEach necklace a contiguous block in rotation order, complements aligned. 1,516 crossings against 906 for plain rows.
  6. Necklace columnsEach necklace stacked in its own vertical column, alternating pair dead center.
  7. Stratified 12-gonEvery necklace flattened onto its own line in 12-gon height order and made symmetric. 990 crossings.
  8. Stratified arcsThree-in-a-row split around the alternating pair, strata bent into nested arcs that shorten total edge length. 1,076 crossings.
  9. Necklace rings on arcsStates drawn as shell patterns, each necklace in rotation order with rings wrapping off both edges. 1,164 crossings.
  10. Compact necklace ringsThin and tall rows one glyph apart, searched for fewest crossings. 988 crossings.
  11. Shell codex with pathfindingClosed necklace rings stacked between the poles, bowed unit edges, and cheapest-path search with configurable rotation and unit costs. 1,072 crossings.
